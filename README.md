# AFK Helper

A RuneLite plugin for staying on top of AFK activities without watching the screen.

## Features

- **Alerts** for low HP and Prayer, monster health, and an approaching idle logout, with optional screen flash, sound and desktop notifications.
- **AFK status** that counts time since your last mouse click or key press.
- **Skilling monitoring** that warns when you stop woodcutting, mining, fishing, cooking, firemaking, agility or hunting.
- **Session overlay** showing duration, XP and XP per hour, kills, damage, supplies used, items gained, profit and combat cost.
- **Monster scanner** to pick which nearby monsters to track.
- **Activity highlighting** that marks nearby trees, rocks, fishing spots and similar activities by whether you have the level for them.

Everything is configured from the AFK Helper side panel or the plugin's settings.
